Selected Projects

01 · Finalist

NASA HUNCH — Lunar Regolith Separation

Designed a device that separates ice from lunar regolith so astronauts can produce water, fuel, and breathable oxygen from lunar resources. Simulated motion in Fusion 360, validated it with a sensor-equipped cardboard prototype, then built manufacturable CAD, 3D-printed parts, and Arduino code.

The functional prototype separated ice from a regolith replica and was selected as a finalist in a nationwide engineering competition.

02 · Automation

Spring Basket Conveyor Line

Designed and operated a conveyor-driven automated assembly line with integrated sensors and a custom claw mechanism, programming motion logic to synchronize part handling in real time.

The system assembled spring baskets with consistent accuracy and speed during timed production runs.

ConveyorSensorsMotion logic
03 · Assistive Tech

Low-Cost Powered Knee Prosthetic

Built a powered knee prototype for controlled flexion and extension for above-the-knee amputees, using accessible materials. PLA parts printed via FDM, with a linear actuator, hinge-linkage mechanism, and DPDT switch for bidirectional control.

Achieved smooth, repeatable motion under light load and mapped out clear improvements for future iterations.

CADFDMActuators
04 · Structures

Go-Kart Upright & Suspension

Designed and analyzed a Formula-style go-kart upright to withstand braking, cornering, and vertical impact loads, using free-body diagrams, hand calculations, and SolidWorks Simulation.

The AISI 4130 design stayed below yield under combined worst-case loading, with a minimum factor of safety of 1.06 at localized mounting holes. Hand calculations closely matched the FEA.
